Reading the wellhead stack bottom-up

Housing, spools, hangers and packoffs in one load-and-pressure path

Stack reading should not begin with memorising names; first trace casing strings, loads, annuli and pressure boundaries, then identify the components.

24

After this lesson, you can

  • Read a stack by function.
  • Distinguish hanger from packoff.
  • Trace annuli on a schematic.

The real question

Where does each load, pressure and annulus terminate?

Bottom-up: the housing establishes the first structural boundary; subsequent spools accept strings; hangers carry load and packoffs isolate annular spaces.

01Mechanical load
02Pressure boundary
03Production path
04Annulus / control

Technical model

01

Housing

The lower foundation and initial casing/surface interface; later loads must follow the designed path into it and the foundation.

02

Spool

A body with upper/lower connections, internal bowl and outlets that establishes another suspension and annulus-control stage.

03

Hanger / packoff

The hanger carries load; the packoff closes a pressure boundary. They may be integrated or separate assemblies.

Learning check

Reason through the operating case

If the hanger carries load but the packoff is absent, what is complete and what remains?

Answer and reasoning

Mechanical suspension is established but annulus pressure isolation is incomplete; next steps depend on the approved procedure and well-control status.
