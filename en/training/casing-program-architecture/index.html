Casing-programme architecture

From conductor to production casing and liners

A casing programme is a telescoping sequence of hole sections and strings designed around subsurface hazards, setting depths and completion needs.

14

After this lesson, you can

  • State the broad roles of conductor, surface, intermediate and production strings.
  • Explain the difference between a casing string and a liner.
  • Read the relationship between hole size, casing OD, drift/ID and clearance.

The real question

Why does well diameter reduce in stages, and what determines where each string ends?

Read outside-in: a larger hole is drilled first, casing is installed, and the next assembly must pass through the previous string ID. Each shoe becomes the gateway to the next section.

01Conductor · shallow support
02Surface · control foundation
03Intermediate · hazard isolation
04Production / liner · completion boundary

Technical model

01

Conductor and surface strings

The conductor supports shallow unconsolidated ground. Surface casing also protects shallow water and establishes the pressure and equipment foundation for deeper drilling.

02

Intermediate string

Intermediate casing isolates incompatible pressure windows, loss zones, salt, unstable shale or other trouble intervals. A well may require more than one intermediate string.

03

Production casing or liner

Production casing is the smallest principal boundary near or across the target interval and must withstand completion and life-cycle loads. A liner does not return to surface; it is suspended from the previous string.

04

Selecting setting depth

A shoe should be placed in competent formation before the next hazard is exposed. Depth alone is insufficient; pore pressure, fracture gradient, stability, kick tolerance and completion objectives are assessed together.

Operational sequence

Each stage establishes the boundary for the next

  1. 01Conductor↓
  2. 02Surface↓
  3. 03Intermediate↓
  4. 04Production / liner↓
  5. 05Tubing and completion

Bridge to wellhead equipment

Where does this appear in the wellhead?

String sequence determines how many suspension and sealing stages the wellhead needs. Section A, B or C is not a universal manufacturer-independent name; always read the casing programme with the approved GA drawing and vendor nomenclature.

Glossary

Terms that must remain precise

Casing shoe
The lower end of a casing string and the reference point for the next drilled section.
Liner
A string that extends upward from depth but does not reach surface and is suspended from the previous casing.
Drift diameter
A verified internal clearance used to confirm passage of a specified tool or assembly.

Learning check

Reason through the operating case

If a production liner does not return to surface, where is its load transferred and why does overlap matter?

Answer and reasoning

A liner is suspended from the preceding casing by a liner hanger. Its overlap and cement/seal system must provide the designed load transfer, pressure isolation and test path.
