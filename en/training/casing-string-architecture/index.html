Casing-string architecture

Conductor, surface, intermediate and production casing, plus liners

String names do not merely indicate size; each string has a role in the pressure, stability and isolation architecture.

After this lesson, you can

  • Explain the general role of each string family.
  • Distinguish a full string from a liner.
  • Connect reducing diameters to wellhead interfaces.

The real question

Why do casing-string count, depth and naming differ from well to well?

Casing architecture is a staged response to well-path hazards, not a fixed diameter table. Each string enables safer drilling of the next section.

Working model

01

Conductor and surface strings

The conductor stabilises the shallow opening and establishes an initial reference. Surface casing generally protects shallow intervals and water resources while supporting later control.

02

Intermediate casing

Intermediate casing isolates troublesome pressure, stability or loss intervals. A well may require several intermediate strings or none.

03

Production casing

Production casing provides the principal completion framework and long-term access to the target interval. Its name does not mean production starts automatically.

04

Liner and tieback

A liner is suspended downhole and does not extend to surface. A tieback can continue its connection upward; selection is well-design specific.

Bridge to well operations

Where does this sit on the route to wellhead work?

Every string reaching surface needs suspension, sealing and load transfer. That need explains the A, B and C sections and the casing-head/spool family.

Glossary

Terms that must remain precise

Casing string
A continuous assembly of casing installed for a well section.
Liner
A string suspended from a previous casing string without extending to surface.
Casing shoe
The lower end of a casing string and the starting reference for the next section.

Learning check

Reason through the answer

Why does a liner normally stop below surface, and what interface requirement follows?

Answer and reasoning

It covers a deeper interval without a full-length string to surface; a liner hanger and, when required, a tieback provide the necessary interfaces.
