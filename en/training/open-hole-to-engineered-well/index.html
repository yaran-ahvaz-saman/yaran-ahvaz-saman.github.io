From open hole to an engineered well

Why open hole requires casing, cement and barriers

In most well architectures, open hole is staged access. An engineered well must isolate required formations, stabilise the path and control unintended flow, although some final intervals may use a designed open-hole completion.

After this lesson, you can

  • Separate the role of casing from the role of cement.

The real question

Why can the whole path not normally be drilled to total depth and left uncased for production?

In conventional programmes, each section solves part of the path and is then stabilised with casing and cement so the next, smaller section can be drilled within controlled boundaries.

Working model

01

Mechanical stability

Casing provides a continuous steel path and supports vulnerable intervals, but it does not by itself seal the external annulus.

02

Zonal isolation

Cement is placed to fill the designed annular interval, support the pipe and restrict unwanted communication between zones with different fluids or pressures.

03

Barriers and verification

Steel presence or pumped volume alone does not prove barrier performance. Test boundaries, placement evidence and acceptance criteria must be defined.

04

Staged construction

After each section is secured, a smaller bit passes through the installed casing. Wellbore and string diameters therefore reduce, with linked tolerances.

Bridge to well operations

Where does this sit on the route to wellhead work?

Each string ultimately transfers load and pressure to surface equipment. This is where casing hangers, casing heads and spools begin to enter the pathway.

Glossary

Terms that must remain precise

Open hole
A drilled interval not yet lined with casing.
Zonal isolation
Restriction of fluid or pressure communication between subsurface zones.
Well barrier
An element or envelope with defined performance criteria that prevents unintended flow.

Learning check

Reason through the answer

Which element creates the internal steel path, and which restricts unwanted annular communication?

Answer and reasoning

Casing creates the steel path; properly placed annular cement provides zonal isolation and external support.
