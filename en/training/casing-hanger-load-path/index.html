Casing hangers and the load path

How is a casing string suspended, sealed and tested at the wellhead?

The casing hanger transfers string load into the housing or spool; the packoff creates an annulus pressure boundary and the designed retention system controls movement.

16

After this lesson, you can

  • Trace the load path from casing through hanger into the body.
  • Explain the annulus volume and packoff function.
  • Distinguish a landed hanger from a verified seal.

The real question

When kilometres of casing hang in a well, how are weight, pressure and thermal movement managed at the wellhead?

Read three functions separately: suspension for load, sealing for pressure and retention for unwanted movement. One assembly may perform several functions, but the terms are not synonyms.

01Conductor · shallow support
02Surface · control foundation
03Intermediate · hazard isolation
04Production / liner · completion boundary

Technical model

01

Landing profile and hanger

The hanger lands on its designed shoulder or profile and transfers axial string load into the body. Slip or mandrel type, installation method and load capacity come from manufacturer documentation.

02

Packoff and pressure boundary

The packoff seals the annular space between hanger/casing and body as designed. Surface condition, landing location, seal energisation and pressure direction belong to the installation procedure.

03

Annulus access

Side outlets support monitoring, bleeding, testing or managing a defined annulus. Each outlet must be mapped to the correct volume using the schematic and GA drawing; guessing the path is unsafe.

04

Lockdown, testing and records

Some systems include separate lockdown or energising mechanisms. Setting sequence, test pressure, hold time and acceptance criteria must be recorded; visual appearance cannot replace test records.

Operational sequence

Each stage establishes the boundary for the next

  1. 01Land hanger↓
  2. 02Transfer load↓
  3. 03Install packoff↓
  4. 04Lock / energise↓
  5. 05Test and record

Bridge to wellhead equipment

Where does this appear in the wellhead?

This is the operational entry point into wellhead equipment. The next lesson distinguishes casing heads, casing spools and tubing heads by the pressure boundaries and strings they accommodate.

Glossary

Terms that must remain precise

Casing hanger
A component that suspends casing in a housing or spool and transfers its load.
Packoff
A sealing assembly that establishes a pressure boundary in a defined annulus.
Load shoulder
A designed surface that transfers hanger load into the body.

Learning check

Reason through the operating case

The hanger has landed, but the packoff test shows pressure decay. Can the installation be accepted solely because load transfer occurred?

Answer and reasoning

No. Suspension and sealing are separate acceptance functions. The string may be mechanically supported while the annulus pressure boundary remains unacceptable; troubleshoot, correct and retest according to procedure.
