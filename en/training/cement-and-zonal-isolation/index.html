Cement and zonal isolation

The sheath that turns steel pipe into a well barrier

Cement fills the annulus to support load, separate formations and restrict unwanted flow. Placement quality matters as much as slurry formulation.

15

After this lesson, you can

  • Separate primary cementing objectives from remedial squeeze work.
  • Explain the basic placement path from casing shoe into the annulus.
  • Distinguish pressure-test evidence from cement-evaluation evidence.

The real question

Why does sound steel pipe alone not guarantee zonal isolation?

Treat the barrier as three boundaries: formation–cement, the cement sheath itself, and cement–casing. Weakness at any boundary can create a pressure or flow path.

01Conductor · shallow support
02Surface · control foundation
03Intermediate · hazard isolation
04Production / liner · completion boundary

Technical model

01

Preparation and displacement

Mud conditioning, compatible spacers, centralisation and displacement rate affect mud removal and cement contact. A good slurry placed poorly does not create a good barrier.

02

Bottom plug, top plug and shoe

Plugs separate fluids and indicate displacement milestones. Cement travels down casing, exits at the shoe and rises in the annulus; job-specific details come from the approved programme.

03

Top of cement and WOC

Designed cement height must match isolation and structural objectives. Before subsequent load or pressure, Wait on Cement time and acceptance criteria are controlled by the programme.

04

Verification, not assumption

Returns, volumes, pressures and plug bump are operational data; pressure tests and, when required, cement-evaluation logs provide different evidence. No single signal answers every integrity question.

Operational sequence

Each stage establishes the boundary for the next

  5. 05Evaluate and test

Bridge to wellhead equipment

Where does this appear in the wellhead?

A wellhead seal may close an annulus at surface, but it does not replace downhole zonal isolation. Annulus pressure must be interpreted with cement history, seal status and operating limits.

Glossary

Terms that must remain precise

Spacer
A compatible intermediate fluid used to separate fluids and assist mud removal ahead of cement.
Wait on cement
The controlled period for cement to develop required properties before further work.
Top of cement
The evaluated upper extent of cement in the relevant annulus.

Learning check

Reason through the operating case

After cementing, the plug bumped as planned but returns were lower than expected. Can complete isolation be confirmed from the successful bump alone?

Answer and reasoning

No. A bump confirms a displacement event, not complete isolation. The volume discrepancy may indicate losses or a different placement outcome and must be assessed using job data, pressure testing and the programme’s evaluation method.
