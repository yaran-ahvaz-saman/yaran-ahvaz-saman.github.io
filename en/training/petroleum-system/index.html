What is a petroleum system?

From hydrocarbon generation to a preserved accumulation

A petroleum system is not a parts list. It connects source, migration, reservoir, trap, seal and preservation in space and geological time.

02Generation and expulsion

After this lesson, you can

  • Separate the principal elements and processes of a petroleum system.
  • Explain why timing can make or break an accumulation.
  • Distinguish generation, charge, accumulation and preservation.

The real question

If a source rock generated petroleum, why might no producible field exist?

Treat the petroleum system as an interdependent chain. Each link is necessary but insufficient; the links must operate in the right place and sequence.

Working model

01

Source and maturity

Source rock needs suitable organic matter and an adequate thermal history. Maturity does not place oil in a reservoir; it turns potential into generation.

02

Expulsion and migration

Petroleum must leave the fine pore network of its source and then move through carrier beds or suitable fractures. A poor pathway can limit reservoir charge.

03

Reservoir, trap and seal

Reservoir rock provides storage and transmissibility, the trap provides geometry, and the seal restricts further escape. These are not synonyms.

04

Timing and preservation

If migration predates trap formation, charge may be lost. After accumulation, faulting, erosion or seal failure can still breach the system.

Bridge to well operations

Where does this sit on the route to wellhead work?

Before well design, the target unit and overlying intervals must be understood. That model supports zonal separation and later casing-setting decisions.

Glossary

Terms that must remain precise

Charge
Delivery of generated and migrated petroleum into a trap or reservoir.
Carrier bed
A permeable unit that provides a route for longer-range migration.
Seal rock
A low-transmissibility unit that restricts petroleum escape from a trap.

Learning check

Reason through the answer

A trap formed after the main migration event. Despite suitable source, reservoir and seal, what is the most likely system failure?

Answer and reasoning

Charge and trap timing are mismatched; petroleum may have passed through or escaped before the containment geometry existed.
