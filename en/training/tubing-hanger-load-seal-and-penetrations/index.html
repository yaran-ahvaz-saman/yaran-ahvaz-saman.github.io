Tubing hanger: load, seal and penetrations

A compact component with three independent duties

The tubing hanger suspends the string, separates tubing bore from annulus and, in selected completions, carries control or cable penetrations.

19

After this lesson, you can

  • Separate load, seal and lockdown functions.
  • Compare mandrel and slip-type concepts.
  • Explain penetration risk.

The real question

Why does mechanical landing alone not prove sealing integrity?

Verify three duties separately: the load shoulder carries weight, the seal establishes a pressure boundary, and the lockdown—where designed—limits upward movement. Every penetration adds another boundary.

02Pressure boundary

Technical model

01

Mandrel and slip-type

A mandrel hanger forms part of the string with defined connections; a slip-type can grip around tubing. Selection depends on system, operation and manufacturer procedure.

02

Seal and lockdown

The seal contains differential pressure; lockdown addresses upward or thermal loads where required. One does not prove the other.

03

Control-line penetration

A cable or hydraulic line must cross the hanger without creating a leak path; termination, seal and testing are part of the completion design.

Field application

What does this change in the field?

Any landing indication should be confirmed by the approved combination of weight, position, running tool and pressure test.

Glossary

Key terms

Load shoulder
Surface transferring hanger load to the body.
Lockdown
Mechanism restraining upward hanger movement.

Learning check

Reason through the operating case

A hanger carries load but fails its pressure test; is increasing lockdown the definitive fix?

Answer and reasoning

No. Inspect seat, seal, debris, damage, penetrations and test method; added force without diagnosis can worsen damage.
