Corporate identity

YAS—oilfield experience organised for today’s work

Yaran Ahvaz Saman is a privately held company from Ahvaz. We bring operating experience, project coordination and technical learning together to address practical wellhead, field-execution and industrial-supply requirements.

YAS / OUR STORY

From field experience to an organised capability

YAS connects operational experience, project management and knowledge transfer. Its development explains both its services and its approach to working with clients.

01

Experience before the company name

Before YAS existed as an organisation, its technical culture was shaped in the oilfields of southern Iran: well operations, field leadership, safety discipline and the patience to document work properly. That experience remains a reference point, not a slogan.

02

2016: turning experience into a working company

Yaran Ahvaz Saman was established to give that accumulated know-how a dependable structure. The aim was straightforward: connect a client’s operating need with a crew, a plan and an accountable route to delivery—not simply offer isolated technical advice.

03

Wellhead work became the proving ground

Wellhead installation, commissioning, testing and maintenance became the discipline through which YAS refined its operating method. Experience at Yadavaran was an important chapter. Crews, specialist tooling and work practices matured together around a simple expectation: installation is incomplete until it has been tested, recorded and properly handed over.

04

Knowledge that can travel beyond one crew

YAS treats field learning as an asset that should outlast an individual assignment. Technical books by Kamal Deilami, classroom sessions and practical wellhead instruction help make experience usable for engineers and crews. Today, field delivery, project supply and technical learning reinforce one another rather than operating as separate lines of work.
